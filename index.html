How the Stripe plugin works

One drop-in script connects your vendor's site to your Stripe & PayPal backend

Vendors never touch Stripe keys or PCI card fields. They embed relay-plugin.js, your Relay backend creates the real Stripe Checkout session, and verified webhooks handle order fulfillment and payout accounting automatically.

STEP 01
Vendor embeds script

The vendor pastes relay-plugin.js and adds data-relay-checkout onto any Buy button.

STEP 02
Relay creates Stripe session

Your Node server calls stripe.checkout.sessions.create() using your Stripe secret key and attaches vendor metadata.

STEP 03
Stripe verifies & webhooks

Customer completes 3DS2 card/Apple Pay checkout. Stripe posts a signed event to /api/stripe/webhook.

STEP 04
Commission & vendor payout

Relay deducts your commission, notifies the vendor's store to fulfill, and queues their net payout in Admin.
