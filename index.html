How the Stripe plugin works

One script on the product page opens Stripe or PayPal

A vendor pastes relay-plugin.js on a product page and never handles card numbers or Stripe keys. Relay creates the Checkout session, Stripe confirms the payment, and the webhook records the order and the vendor’s crypto cashout.

STEP 01
Vendor embeds script

The vendor pastes relay-plugin.js and adds data-relay-checkout onto any Buy button.

STEP 02
Relay creates Stripe session

Relay creates the Stripe Checkout session for that vendor and sends the customer to Stripe.

STEP 03
Stripe verifies & webhooks

Customer completes 3DS2 card/Apple Pay checkout. Stripe posts a signed event to /api/stripe/webhook.

STEP 04
Commission & vendor payout

Relay keeps its fee, marks the order paid, and adds the vendor’s net to their crypto cashout balance.


              

              

            

            

              

                

                  
Zero PCI burden on the vendor

                  
The vendor adds the script and a buy button. The button opens checkout, Relay creates the Stripe or PayPal session, and the customer pays there.

                

                

                  
Relay creates the Checkout session

                  
Relay calls Stripe with the platform key, tags the session with the vendor, the fee, and the vendor net, then sends the customer to Stripe.

                

                

                  
Cryptographically verified fulfillment & ledger

                  
Stripe signs every webhook with STRIPE_WEBHOOK_SECRET. Relay verifies the signature, marks the order paid, dispatches an HMAC webhook to the vendor site, and queues their net payout.
